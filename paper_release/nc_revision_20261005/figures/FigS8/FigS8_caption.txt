Supplementary Fig. S8. A three-site accounting experiment conserves workload and clean supply across routing and power policies. a, Absolute uncovered GPU electricity for local execution and two assumed added-network budgets. b, Total GPU electricity under the same fixed 5,762.47 kWh clean-supply budget. The experiment uses all 16,803,695 Azure coding arrivals and the same finite 3,360,749 background job units. The remain-on and backfill policies retain all installed GPU idle power; consolidation changes awake counts. c, Changes in the US, Germany and Japan site powers for the first 48 hours under 500 ms routing with GPUs remaining on, relative to local execution; site changes sum to zero each hour. d, Cumulative completion of the same background pool, including its daily release schedule. Background schedules differ within a day and converge at daily deadlines. The three 500 ms policies have uncovered fractions of 15.80%, 13.60% and 9.22%, respectively; each fraction is divided by its own total electricity in b. Origin assignment, design capacity, power interpolation and network delays are controlled assumptions. Reconstructed 2025 wind/solar profiles are paired with 2024 arrival hours. Results quantify modeled power accounting and do not certify request-level service or measured facility consumption.
