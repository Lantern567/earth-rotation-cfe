# Economic sensitivity on a fixed execution ledger

The economic calculation keeps the three-site execution schedules and installed/awake capacities unchanged. For each local and 500 ms policy, a volumetric energy-cost scenario multiplies every site's modeled GPU electricity by a specified price, then sums all sites. Uniform prices are 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20 USD/kWh. Two nonuniform vectors assign US/Germany/Japan prices of 0.05/0.10/0.20 USD/kWh and the reverse. All prices are scenario assumptions. These calculations do not estimate actual facility bills or contracted electricity procurement, and omit PPA rights, tariff structure, egress, hardware reservations and taxes.

For remain-on and finite backfill, routing preserves total modeled electricity. Uniform-price costs therefore remain identical to the corresponding local baseline. Consolidation reduces modeled electricity from 3387.24454 to 3378.33554 kWh, giving uniform-price cost decreases of 0.44545, 0.89090 and 1.78180 USD over this one-week experiment. Under the ascending site-price vector, 500 ms remain-on changes volumetric cost by +11.22516 USD and consolidation by −18.23337 USD; under the reverse vector the changes are −7.97329 and +26.35189 USD. These differences describe a frozen schedule, without price-responsive optimization or real contractual savings.

For the 500 ms remain-on versus local comparison, routing avoids 307.020927 kWh of same-hour uncovered electricity. Let v be an explicitly assumed shadow value of 30, 100 or 200 USD/MWh. The hypothetical value of avoided uncovered energy is v times 0.307020927 MWh. Subtracting the scenario volumetric cost change and dividing by approximately 10.33 million routed request-equivalents gives the additional per-request routing-fee threshold. Under uniform prices, the thresholds are 8.915146×10⁻⁷, 2.971715×10⁻⁶ and 5.943431×10⁻⁶ USD per routed request-equivalent. The assumed shadow value cannot be interpreted as an actual electricity bill reduction. For the ascending site prices with v=30 USD/MWh, the net margin is negative, with a threshold of −1.949901×10⁻⁷ USD/request-equivalent; this scenario provides no positive margin for an additional fee.
